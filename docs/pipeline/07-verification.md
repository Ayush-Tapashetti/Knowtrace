## Findings (honest)

1. **Diagnosis is inconclusive too often on a cold start.** In the browser run, a learner whose hidden gap was "Equivalent fractions" ended INCONCLUSIVE after 4 probes (the probes spread over unrelated ancestors and hit the gap last). A gap at "Adding fractions with the same denominator" worked end to end. The eval agrees: 35% of simulated students miss top-1, and most listed misses are "inconclusive" and mostly deep gaps (depth 4 to 13). Mitigations to try: allow a fifth probe, or weight probes toward the failed item's nearer ancestors.
2. **Graph and flat BKT predict the next answer equally well** (AUC 0.71, Brier 0.19 for both). The graph's advantage is root-cause diagnosis, not next-answer prediction. The Evaluation page now ticks both, and baselines are shown as n/a for probes instead of a misleading 0.00.
3. **Calibration is under-confident in the middle** (ECE 0.097, inside the 0.12 gate).
4. **The graph overview is dense at 47 concepts.** Colours read at a glance, labels need zoom; the table view lists everything.

## Not verified

- AI rewording against a real provider (needs an API key and network): only the fake client path ran. The "Reworded by AI" badge has not been seen live.
- Screen reader and keyboard walkthrough with real assistive tech; only roles, labels and the table alternatives were checked.
- Browsers other than Chromium; phone widths checked only for horizontal overflow at 390 px.
- Load, and persistence across a server restart.

## Ready to ship? 

As a hackathon demo: yes, with the caveat in finding 1. The core loop, contract, fallbacks and privacy checks are verified offline. Before a real pilot: raise the diagnosis success rate, run the provider path once with a key, and do an accessibility pass. P1 items from `02-scope.md` (teacher heatmap, spaced review, import, PDF export, dark mode) are untouched.
